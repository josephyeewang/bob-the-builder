# Specification delta

Two existing checkers become more honest about evidence: explicitly requested but unavailable source fidelity cannot pass, and observed task coverage can validate the actual structured result for the expected run. Legacy coverage is supported without upgrading its evidential claim.

Existing templates and lenses gain concrete tests of applicability, usefulness, authority, semantic preservation, completion and recovery. They retain their existing owners and gates; no parallel-owned mode or runtime changes. The operative contracts live in the checker docstrings, audit evidence guide and affected templates, not here.
